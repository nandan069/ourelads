Micro-Dramas: The New Language of Brand Communication

From the Ourel Ads studio.

Advertising · Ourel Ads

India already thinks in serials. Micro-dramas simply move that habit to the phone — short arcs, real situations, a brand sitting inside the story instead of interrupting it.

We use this form for credit, savings, learning and everyday commerce because those categories are emotional long before they are transactional. A character missing a due date will always beat a bullet list of APR.
