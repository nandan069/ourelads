Who We Are

A growth partner, not a vendor.

Founded in 2022 and headquartered in New Delhi, Ourel Ads brings research, strategy, concept, scripting, casting, production, editing and delivery under one roof.

OUREL stands for Outreach, Understanding, Reach, Engagement and Loyalty. That is how we work: understand people and culture, take the right message to the right audience, create work they want to watch, and turn attention into lasting brand relationships.

We’ve partnered with brands including Infinix, GoodScore, Seekho, Brands.live, GoCredit, Vyapar, Bansal Group, FatakPay, Bachatt and Jar — helping them grow faster in competitive markets.
